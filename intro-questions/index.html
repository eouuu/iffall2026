  5. Designing for a screen requires thinking more about how someone interacts with and navigates through the design. You also have to consider different screen sizes and aspect ratios, while a printed design has a more fixed format.

  6. Nashallery's Booth is an example of effective design because it turns a familiar photo booth into something interactive on the web. I like that the design is simple but still feels playful and want to interact with it.

  7. Wikipedia exemplifies effective communication because it ogranizes large amounts of inormation that is easy to scan and navigate. The heading, links, and consistent page structure make it easy to find specfic information.
