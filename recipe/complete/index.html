Miso Tomato Burrata Pasta

30 min 1 serving Contains dairy

A rich and savory tomato pasta made with cherry tomatoes, garlic, shallot, miso, and creamy burrata.

Ingredients

  • 1 shallot
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1 box cherry tomatoes
  • 1 ball burrata
  • Spaghettini or pasta of choice
  • Miso paste
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Garlic powder
  • Chili flakes
  • Rosemary

Instructions

  1. Bring a pot of salted water to a boil and cook the pasta according to the package instructions.
  2. Finely chop the shallot and garlic and cut the cherry tomatoes in half.
  3. Cook the shallot and garlic until softened and fragrant.
  4. Add the tomatoes and cook until they soften and begin to form a sauce.
  5. Stir in the miso paste with a small amount of pasta water.
  6. Add the cooked pasta and toss until completely coated in the sauce.
  7. Transfer to a bowl, add the burrata, and finish with pepper, chili flakes, or rosemary.

Equipment

  • Large pot
  • Frying pan or skillet
  • Cutting board
  • Knife
  • Wooden spoon or spatula
  • Colander

Serving + Storage

Serve immediately with the burrata on top. Finish with black pepper, chili flakes, or rosemary if desired.
